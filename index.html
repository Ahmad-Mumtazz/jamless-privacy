Jamless: Traffic Jam Puzzle

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Jamless: Traffic Jam Puzzle game ("Jamless", "the app", "we") uses and why. Jamless has no user accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, cars you own, settings and whether you bought "Remove ads") is saved only on your device. We do not receive or store it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Jamless is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version), and
  • how you interact with ads (for example impressions and taps).

This information is collected by Google, not by us. How Google uses it is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings.

In-app purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase was made.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the game opens your device's share menu with a short text about your progress. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

Children

Jamless is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

We do not run servers that store player data. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
